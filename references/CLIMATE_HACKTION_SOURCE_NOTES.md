# Official Climate Hack-tion source navigation

This document indexes the included official reference PDFs. Treat the PDFs as authoritative, and verify specific clauses before quoting externally.

## `Participant Guide.pdf`
- Event: online Climate Hack-tion 2026, 2–4 October; theme **Build for 2035**, for eligible students and teams of 3–5.
- Start of substantive project work: after 9:00 AM Sydney local time on Friday, 2 October 2026. Research/ideation allowed beforehand; prebuilt work is restricted by the guide. Verify compliance for each asset.
- Tracks include electrification; zero waste/methane reduction; resilient cities/buildings; green industrialisation; climate awareness.
- Judging: COP31 alignment 30%, build quality 30%, creativity 20%, presentation clarity 20%.
- Hackathon submission: project title, summary, target users/problem, impact and pitch, team members and roles, <=2-minute video, public source/files, tools/data/AI disclosure, evidence of build during hackathon.
- Deadline: Sunday 4 October 2026 at **9 PM Sydney time (AEDT)** (Sydney changes from AEST to AEDT this weekend); user time may differ.
- The included guide is visually rendered/scanned, so it may not be searchable as text. Inspect pages visually or use appropriate PDF reading tools; do not treat text-extraction failures as missing content.
