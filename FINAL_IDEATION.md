# WasteWise — FINAL CURRENT IDEATION (Oct 3, 2026)

This is the latest project decision set. It supersedes early Climate Chaos ideas aimed at all ages, and the intermediate Years 7–9 plan visible in the conversation exports.

## Final game
**WasteWise — The Eco Crew Adventure**: Grade 4 Victorian Geography learning through an original, animated, story-led, walkable 2D school adventure. Target: Victorian Year/Grade 4 students, roughly ages 9–10; secondary users: teachers.

**Original game identity**: Sunny School; the player joins the Eco Crew; **Milo the Eco Mouse** (original friendly mouse with leaf explorer hat and teal backpack) follows the player and narrates; supporting characters: Principal Maple, Chef Sunny, Professor Sprout. Voice cast uses *original* lively cartoon performances rather than Mickey Mouse or Disney character likeness/voice imitation.

**Game style**: Option B, walkable school adventure with selective story cutscenes and options for dialogue. Walking/running, interactions with places and characters, animated responses and smooth transitions. Game should not reduce to reading cards or taking quizzes.

### Chapters
1. **The Resource Mystery** — walk through grounds/garden, notice a leaking tap/resource waste, inspect evidence, play a sequencing/resource-saving mini-game, see consequences and explanations.
2. **Canteen Chaos / Waste Maze** — help Chef Sunny with waste at lunch; navigate a maze, collect/transport objects, sort according to stated fictional-school rules, include waste *prevention* and reuse, give feedback and non-punitive retries. Treat maze skill separately from waste-management understanding.
3. **Waste Detective** — interpret a small school map, gather simulated audit clues, interpret/create a chart, recommend a school improvement and justify using evidence.
4. **Final — Save Sunny School** — new, independent, *untimed* 20-mark problem: identify resource/waste issues (4); sustainable actions (5); interpret a map/chart (5); justify proposed improvement (6). Auto-score only deterministic items; teacher-review open explanation.

### Special requirements
- Original, expressive 2D cartoon motion graphics (walk/run/idle/talk/celebrate, animated environments) with keyboard and touch alternatives.
- Voice Option C (multiple characters); ElevenLabs pre-rendered clips only if provided/licensed, otherwise a labelled optional speech-synthesis fallback. Dialogue has captions/mute/replay. Never put ElevenLabs secret keys in browser JavaScript.
- Curriculum: Victorian F–10 Curriculum Version 2.0, Levels 3–4 Geography, especially **VC2HG4K09** (sustainable use of natural resources and management of waste); validate full text and linked geography inquiry skills against official VCAA source before claiming complete alignment.
- COP31 tracks: climate awareness/education; zero waste/methane reduction. The source library's five tracks include electrification, waste & methane, resilient cities/buildings, green industrialisation, climate awareness & education.
- Intro may ask only for *optional nickname*, no personal accounts or student identifiers.
- Standalone school browser game, not a clone of Minecraft: Story Mode or any Disney property.

### Hackathon scope
Build a high-quality demonstrable vertical slice first (walkable school hub + mascot + dialogue + a complete scenario + 2 actual interactive mini-game mechanics + consequence/learning + assessment sample). Then expand chapters only when stable. Deliver a public repository, <=2-minute demonstration video, summary, curriculum/impact explanation, tools/AI disclosures; consult official participant guide for exact submission fields and dates.
