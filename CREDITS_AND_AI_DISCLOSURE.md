## Facts used in the game (to verify or cite)
- COP31 targets (from the bundled *Climate Hacktion Library*): "Halve the growth in global municipal solid waste by 2035"; "Work toward climate action education for all by 2035".
- Food waste in landfill breaks down without oxygen and produces **methane**, a greenhouse gas that traps much more heat than carbon dioxide over the short term. Composting with air avoids this landfill methane.
- Red / yellow / green lid colours match many Victorian council bins (general waste / recycling / food & garden organics). The game states that rules differ between places, and teaches only the fictional Sunny School and Hilltop School rules.
- Some electricity is generated by burning coal and gas, which adds greenhouse gases. (The game makes no numerical claims about energy or water savings.)
- All audit numbers and school data are **fictional** teaching examples.
