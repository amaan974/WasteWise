# Teacher guide — WasteWise: The Eco Crew Adventure

**Audience:** Victorian Grade 4 (Levels 3–4 Geography). **Time:** about 35–45 minutes for the full game, or one chapter per lesson.

**Curriculum focus:** Victorian Curriculum F–10 Version 2.0, Geography Levels 3–4 — **VC2HG4K09**, *sustainability and its application to the use of natural resources and the management of waste* (wording matches a secondary source; please confirm on the official VCAA site), plus geographical inquiry skills (questions, collecting and representing data, maps, conclusions, proposed actions). This is one targeted unit, not the whole Levels 3–4 Geography curriculum. See [`CURRICULUM_ALIGNMENT_MATRIX.md`](CURRICULUM_ALIGNMENT_MATRIX.md).

## Learning intentions
Students will:
1. Identify natural resources (water, energy, materials) being wasted in a familiar place, and explain why it matters.
2. Choose sustainable, safe actions — prevent waste first, then reuse, recycle/compost, and use landfill last; report faults to an adult.
3. Explain why food in landfill is a problem (methane) and how composting helps.
4. Use a compass map, collect and classify data, build and read a bar chart.
5. Propose an improvement and justify it with evidence.

## Suggested lesson plan (45 min)
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 5 min | Hook: "Where does the rubbish from our lunch go?" Show the title screen and Milo. |
| 10 min | **Chapter 1** (garden): resources, the tap decision, the puzzles. |
| 10 min | **Chapter 2** (canteen + waste maze). Pause after the lunch choice: *Which lunch makes less rubbish? Why?* |
| 10 min | **Chapter 3** (Eco Lab): map, audits, bar chart, plan. |
| 8 min | **Final challenge** (untimed — let students finish in the next lesson if needed). |
| 2 min | Reflection: *Which of the game's ideas could work at OUR school? What evidence would we need?* |

**Shorter option:** one chapter per lesson. Use **Esc → Teacher & demo options → Jump to a chapter**.

## The final challenge (20 marks)
- **A · Spot the problems (4, auto-marked):** choose the 4 problems in a picture of a new school.
- **B · Sustainable actions (5, auto-marked):** sort 3 items under Hilltop School's rules; 2 action questions.
- **C · Geographical evidence (5, auto-marked):** chart and compass-map questions.
- **D · Explain your plan (6, teacher-reviewed):** a written plan. Use the **Teacher marking** button on the results screen:
  - 2 — a specific, realistic action
  - 2 — uses evidence (chart/map) or environmental reasoning
  - 2 — clear explanation of who would help, how and why

Different valid plans can earn full marks; please don't mark by keywords alone. The computer **never** awards the 6 teacher marks. The results screen offers **Download report** (.txt) and **Print**.

## Practice evidence (not in the 20 marks)
Each chapter has a 3-question quick check (first-try score recorded), and the waste maze records items sorted right first time. These appear in the report under "Game journey" with the student's story decisions.

## Accuracy and safety notes
- **Sunny School and Hilltop School are fictional.** Their bin rules are stated on screen. Rules differ between councils and schools — localise before real-world activities.
- All audit numbers are **fictional samples**.
- Students are **never** asked to touch real waste. For a real audit, use clean demonstration items and adult supervision.
- Eco points are a game score, **not** a measurement of real carbon or waste reduction.
- The assessment is a practice tool, not an official VCAA grade.

## Accessibility
Everything spoken is shown as text. Voices can be "read aloud" (browser speech), "cartoon chatter" or off. Settings include music/sound volume, text speed, bigger text and reduced motion. It works with keyboard only, mouse only or touch. There is no timer anywhere.

## Privacy
No accounts or real names (optional nickname). Progress is saved only in this browser on this device. Final answers are not stored after the page is closed unless the report is downloaded. Nothing is uploaded. **Shared devices:** use Esc → Teacher & demo options → Reset all progress between students.
