# 2-minute demo script (for the judges' video)

**Before recording:** start the server (`python3 tools/serve.py`) and open <http://localhost:8000> in a large browser window with the sound on. For a clean take, use Esc → Teacher & demo options → **Reset all progress**. To jump straight to a part, use Esc → Teacher & demo options → **Jump to a chapter**. Record with OBS, QuickTime or Loom. Keep it to **2:00 or less**.

| Time | On screen | What to say (suggested) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Title screen with Milo waving | "Grade 4 students learn about waste in theory, but rarely practise the everyday decisions. WasteWise turns those decisions into an adventure, for COP31's *climate education for all* and *waste & methane* goals." |
| 0:12–0:25 | New adventure → nickname → walk with the arrow keys; Milo pops out | "Students walk around Sunny School with Milo the Eco Mouse — our original mascot — and a cast of characters with their own voices. Every line has subtitles." |
| 0:25–0:45 | Ch 1: inspect the dripping tap → choose "Leave it" → the garden wilts → rewind → "Tell the caretaker" | "Choices have visible consequences. Ignore the leak and you see the garden wilt a week later; then time rewinds so they can choose again — no punishment." |
| 0:45–0:55 | Pipe puzzle: connect the rain tank to the beds → rain and blooming garden | "Resource-saving puzzles: route rainwater straight to the roots." |
| 0:55–1:20 | Ch 2 (jump to it): pick a lunch → the maze → carry a banana peel to Landfill → methane explanation → put it in Compost | "Your lunch choice decides how much rubbish appears. In a real maze, students sort each item under the school's rules. Put food in landfill and Milo explains methane, a gas that warms the planet." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Ch 3: map → audit tarp → build the bar chart → choose a plan + evidence → Nude Food banner appears | "Then they think like geographers: read a compass map, audit bins around the school, build a bar chart, and choose an action that matches the evidence." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Final challenge Part A picture → results with 14 auto marks and the teacher-marking button | "The final 20-mark challenge is untimed and set in a new school. 14 marks are auto-marked; the written plan is left for the teacher — we never auto-mark it. Teachers get a downloadable report." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Free play: the greener school | "Victorian Curriculum Levels 3–4 Geography, focused on sustainable resource use and waste. Next step: classroom testing with real Grade 4 students." |

**Tips:** Turn on "Fast" text speed (Esc → Sound & settings) to keep the pace. Mention that schools and data are fictional. Don't claim classroom trials or VCAA approval.
